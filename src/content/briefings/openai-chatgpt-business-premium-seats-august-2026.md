## Key Takeaways

- Premium seats cost $125/month ($100/month billed annually, $1,200/year); Standard remains $25/month ($20/month billed annually)
- Premium removes the five-hour usage cap and delivers 5x more capacity than Standard, per OpenAI's own metric
- Standard and Premium seats can be mixed freely in a single workspace
- The first 10,000 eligible workspace owners who join the waitlist before August 20 and add qualifying seats within 30 days of Premium's launch may receive $100 in credits per seat, up to five seats ($500 max); sign-up alone does not guarantee credits
- Upgrade only users who demonstrably hit usage limits — five times the price needs five times the utilization

OpenAI's new Premium tier for ChatGPT Business removes the five-hour usage cap that throttles extended agentic tasks. At [$125 per user per month](https://openai.com/index/premium-seats-chatgpt-business/) ($100/month billed annually, $1,200/year), it costs five times the Standard seat rate. Workspace owners who join the waitlist before **August 20** may earn up to $500 in credits — a limited-time offset that makes the upgrade decision more immediate than OpenAI's typical rollout pace.

## Who Actually Hits the Five-Hour Cap?

The five-hour limit applies to extended agentic sessions. OpenAI's [announcement](https://openai.com/index/premium-seats-chatgpt-business/) names the core workloads: converting sales data into operating plans, building full campaigns from research, running Codex across large codebases — multi-step tasks that can exhaust a usage block in a single session.

Standard users who hit the cap can add workspace credits ad hoc, but that adds variable billing and admin overhead. Premium converts the problem into a predictable monthly line item — worth $960/year more only if the constrained users hit the limit frequently.

The mix-and-match capability keeps the decision tractable: Standard and Premium seats can coexist in one plan under the same [admin dashboard](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792828-what-is-chatgpt-business). A team with three heavy users and nine occasional users does not need to upgrade all twelve.

## Should You Upgrade Before the August 20 Deadline?

OpenAI's credit promotion runs through August 20. The first 10,000 eligible workspace owners who join the waitlist and add qualifying seats within 30 days of Premium's launch receive $100 in workspace credits per seat, up to five seats — a maximum $500 offset.

Joining the waitlist does not guarantee credits; you must also add qualifying seats within 30 days of Premium's launch, [per the promotion terms](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001420). The offer is not a reason to upgrade users who don't need capacity. It is a reason to run the usage analysis now rather than waiting for general availability.

Concrete steps before August 20:

1. Pull workspace usage reports and identify users who regularly hit the five-hour cap.
2. Calculate the annual delta: $240/user on Standard versus $1,200/user on Premium. The upgrade justifies itself only when productivity loss from cap interruptions exceeds $960/year per user.
3. Join the waitlist only for confirmed cap-constrained users; keep the rest on Standard.

**Operator posture: run a small test.** Join the waitlist for one or two confirmed heavy users; test actual output once seats become available, then decide on broader rollout.

## What Should You Watch at General Availability?

OpenAI has not announced a GA date or confirmed whether the $100/month annual rate holds post-launch. The announcement specifies only higher volume — not different model access — so Premium should not be expected to unlock different AI models than Standard; watch the GA announcement to confirm.

Also track how the [Codex seat restriction](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792828-what-is-chatgpt-business) — no new Codex seats for workspaces that lacked them before June 24 — interacts with Premium code-generation workflows.

## FAQ

### Is Premium a different model, or just more usage?

Based on OpenAI's announcement as of August 12, 2026, Premium provides 5x more usage (per OpenAI's stated metric) and removes the five-hour cap — it does not appear to unlock different AI models from Standard. Both tiers access the same workspace models; Premium removes the volume constraint for the heaviest users.

### What if we miss the August 20 deadline?

OpenAI has not announced when Premium will reach general availability or confirmed post-launch pricing. The disclosed deadline is the credit promotion only: $100 per seat, up to $500 total, for the first 10,000 eligible workspace owners who also add qualifying seats within 30 days. Watch the GA announcement before committing to a broader rollout.

### Our team mostly uses ChatGPT for drafts and quick questions. Do we need Premium?

Per OpenAI's announcement, the five-hour cap is framed around extended agentic sessions — multi-step tasks like converting sales data into operating plans or running Codex across large codebases. OpenAI has not confirmed whether shorter sessions count separately toward the cap. Check your workspace usage data; if no one is hitting the cap, Standard is likely sufficient — but usage reports are a more reliable guide than workload category alone.
