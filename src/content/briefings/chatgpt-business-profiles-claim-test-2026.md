OpenAI now lets eligible companies claim and update business profiles inside ChatGPT, view aggregate mentions and website clicks, and—in narrower cases—publish a customer-facing agent. This is a real distribution test, not proof of a new sales channel. Operators should **claim the profile, verify facts, and run a small measurement test** before funding a broader “AI visibility” program.

**Key takeaways:**

- Business profiles and 28-day engagement insights are in beta.
- Claiming does not improve or control ChatGPT recommendations.
- Business Agents currently serve eligible U.S. online businesses.
- Operators should test factual accuracy and qualified traffic first.

## What did OpenAI launch with ChatGPT business profiles?

On November 10, OpenAI introduced [Businesses in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) in beta. Through Business Hub, an authorized representative can claim a listing, complete identity verification, update business details, and connect a product feed with prices and availability.

The accompanying [setup documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt) says insights cover the previous 28 days and report aggregate website mentions, clicks, and discovery topics. Businesses do not receive individual prompts or customer conversations, and blank metrics may reflect privacy thresholds or incomplete reporting rather than zero activity.

[Availability has boundaries](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt). Profile eligibility varies by location and business type; local stores and restaurants can currently claim only existing U.S. listings. As of November 11, Business Agents are limited to eligible U.S. ecommerce and online-service businesses. They can explain products and policies and send users to a website, but cannot complete purchases, bookings, or order checks in the current experience.

## Why don’t ChatGPT business profiles guarantee recommendations?

OpenAI explicitly says claiming a business does not guarantee greater visibility or direct control over independent recommendations. [Updates can take time to appear](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt), and ChatGPT may continue using other sources. A connected feed therefore improves the information OpenAI can access; it does not buy placement or establish attribution.

That distinction matters because OpenAI also permits businesses to [sponsor agents as ad destinations](https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai/). The company says sponsored placements remain labeled and separate from independent answers, while its [business terms](https://openai.com/policies/businesses-in-chatgpt-terms/) govern profiles, feeds, and agents. Operators should measure unpaid mentions, paid traffic, and agent interactions separately rather than combining them into one “ChatGPT performance” number.

The broader shift is consistent with our [answer-engine optimization analysis](/analysis/answer-engine-optimization-aeo-business-2026/), but this beta replaces some guesswork with platform-provided data. It also differs from [Meta’s channel-native Business Agent](/briefings/meta-business-agent-enterprise-launch-2026/): OpenAI’s current agent is an informational conversation attached to a profile, not a transactional support layer connected to customer orders.

## What should operators test now?

**Run a small test.** Claim the profile using an account the business controls, assign at least two administrators, and document the submitted description, policies, links, and feed. Before publishing an agent, test 20 common customer questions against current prices, availability, return terms, and prohibited claims. OpenAI’s [Business Agents Policy](https://openai.com/policies/business-agents-policy/) requires accurate information and restricts unnecessary or high-risk personal-data collection; treat those rules as a floor, not a quality-control system.

For four weeks, record the same 10–15 discovery prompts, profile mentions, website clicks, and qualified actions on your own site. Do not infer customer intent from discovery topics or a verified badge. Watch next for stable attribution, multi-location management, broader agent eligibility, and evidence that clicks convert—not merely that a business is mentioned.

## FAQs

### Does claiming a profile improve ChatGPT rankings?

No. OpenAI says a claim lets a business manage supplied information but does not guarantee increased visibility or control recommendations.

### Can a Business Agent take orders or bookings?

Not currently. It can answer questions and link to the business website, where the customer completes the action.

### What is the first useful metric?

Track qualified website actions from ChatGPT alongside mentions and clicks. Visibility without accurate answers or downstream action is not business value.
